## Applying the map to cases

Assign the primary domain by what the participant is trying to accomplish. Add a related domain when a case describes a distinct second task. For example, renewing a residence permit belongs to immigration and updating registered residence belongs to civil registration, even when a participant describes both as “sorting out my residence”. Keep ambiguous goals open until the case provides enough context.

Public reference material was checked on 17 November 2026. The revenue agency index was readable through its indexed result and returned HTTP 403 on direct retrieval during this check. The education reference is a dated example of the Unica channel. Treat the links as research starting points for the case mapping and applicability checks in tasks 12.2–12.5. The [research status](phase-02-user-case-sampling.md#collection-status) records participant and case coverage.

## Map everyday phrasing to procedure candidates

Keep the participant’s opening words in the case card. Use what they want to achieve to identify a procedure candidate, and record the clue that supports it. Ask a short follow-up if the document, goal or stage could change the candidate. Keep the relevant candidates open when the answer is unclear, and record both goals when the participant has two distinct tasks.

The Italian and English utterances below are synthetic examples. The corresponding official sources are in the domain table above.

### Phrases that could mean more than one thing

| Everyday phrase | Possible procedures | Clarifying question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Devo sistemare la residenza. / I need to sort out my residence paperwork. | **Cambio di residenza** if the goal is to update the registered address. **Rilascio o rinnovo del permesso di soggiorno** if the goal is to obtain or renew permission to stay. | Devi aggiornare l’indirizzo all’anagrafe o fare una pratica per il permesso di soggiorno? / Are you updating your registered address or dealing with a residence permit? |
| Devo fare le pratiche per la disoccupazione. / I need to do the unemployment paperwork. | **DID** if the goal is to declare availability for work. **NASpI** if the goal is to apply for the unemployment benefit. [INPS · NASpI](https://www.inps.it/it/it/dettaglio-scheda.it.schede-servizio-strumento.schede-servizi.50593.naspi-indennit-mensile-di-disoccupazione.html) | Vuoi chiedere un’indennità o dichiarare che sei disponibile a lavorare? / Are you applying for a benefit or declaring that you’re available for work? |
| Devo fare una pratica per il matrimonio. / I need to do some marriage paperwork. | **Pubblicazioni di matrimonio** if the goal is to publish banns ahead of the wedding. **Trascrizione dell’atto di matrimonio estero** if the goal is to register a marriage already celebrated abroad. [Città di Torino · Registrare il matrimonio celebrato all’estero](https://www.comune.torino.it/servizi/registrare-matrimonio-celebrato-allestero) | Il matrimonio deve ancora celebrarsi? Se è già avvenuto, in quale Paese? / Is the wedding still ahead? If it already took place, in which country? |

For the unemployment example, INPS says the NASpI application also serves as the DID. If the participant mentions both, ask what they have already filed before recording any outstanding step. The procedure candidate then feeds the authority and channel checks in 12.3 and 12.4.

## Identify the responsible authority

Start with the procedure candidate. Record the body that provides the service and the office or institution that handles the relevant stage when the official source identifies them. For an existing case, record the issuing body named in the participant’s acknowledgement or notice and check its role against the source. Ask only for the municipality, region, institution or situation detail that would change the result. Keep the authority or jurisdiction pending when a required detail or an applicable source is missing.

| Domain | Responsibility for the reference task | Detail to clarify |

### Synthetic jurisdiction examples

- For “I moved house”, a move within Parma points to the municipality’s internal address-change procedure. ### Record the channel in a case

For each step, record the purpose (information, filing, assistance, appointment or status), the portal or office, and the official URL or evidence reference. Record what the participant actually tried and its outcome. Label each piece of evidence as published, participant-reported or observed with permission. If the reported route differs from the published one, record both and check the case details before drawing a conclusion. When an appointment appears, record whether the person booked it or received a summons, and which body supplied the instructions. Keep an unclear purpose, office or appointment pending.

For a synthetic permit request already identified as a postal case, the public procedure page is the information source, Sportello Amico is the filing point, and the Questura summons is evidence for the appointment stage. In the synthetic Torino marriage case, the portal receives the request and the municipal office contacts the person about the next step. These examples illustrate the fields. The [collection status](phase-02-user-case-sampling.md#collection-status) records participant coverage for observed routes.
