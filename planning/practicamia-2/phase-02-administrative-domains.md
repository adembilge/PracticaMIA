## Applying the map to cases

Assign the primary domain by what the participant is trying to accomplish. Add a related domain when a case describes a distinct second task. For example, renewing a residence permit belongs to immigration and updating registered residence belongs to civil registration, even when a participant describes both as “sorting out my residence”. Keep ambiguous goals open until the case provides enough context.
