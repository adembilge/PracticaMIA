# User problems

Task 2. Researched 1 November 2026. No interviews have been done. This file records the difficulties that show up in public sources, the assumptions PaperPilot is making, and the questions that would test those assumptions.

This is not the procedure dossier. Stage 2 still has to verify eligibility, documents, and submission steps before any of this becomes a rule.

## What the sources support

A person who changes address inside Milan, or arrives from another Italian municipality, is told to file on the Anagrafe Nazionale site with SPID or CIE, within 20 days of the move. The Comune registers the declaration in the next two working days and then checks that the requirements hold. Legal effect starts on the day of the declaration. A failed check stops the procedure. A false statement to a public official is referred to the judicial authority and can cancel benefits. The same Milan page tells the person to update the TARI housing declaration. The service page is published in Italian, English, Arabic, Chinese, French, Japanese, Greek, Russian, Spanish, and German. Turkish is not in that list.

The national service splits the declaration in two. "Nuova residenza" is for a home where nobody else lives, or where the other people are not family. A group of students sharing a flat is the example given. "Residenza in famiglia esistente" is for moving in with relatives or a partner. Before starting, the person is told to have cadastral details if they own the home, or the lease details and the owner's identity card if they do not. A non-EU citizen must attach the residence permit. The declaration is free. The municipality has up to 45 days to finish the checks. Status is meant to be visible under "Le tue richieste", with email and IO-app notices.

Milan's own support article, for a residence request made with a valid permit, asks for a passport or other valid identity document, the residence title, the tax code, and civil-status records that are translated and legalised when family relationships have to be recorded. It says those civil-status records are not mandatory for the request itself and are necessary for recording the relationship.

In 2022, Municipio 2 recorded 24,233 anagrafe visits, 22.5% of them by foreign users, in a district where 29% of residents were foreign. The civic-services assessor described the practical difficulty as language: people do not always know which papers to bring, from the photo to the certificates, or how to book an appointment.

## Documented difficulties

1. **The person has to pick the right path before the form will make sense.** The Milan page covers an address change inside the city and a move from another municipality. The national form then asks whether the new home is a new residence or an existing family. A shared student flat and a move in with a partner are different declarations. First registration from abroad is a different request again, with its own document rules on the integration portal.

2. **The online door is SPID or CIE.** Milan states that access to the service requires one of those. The 2022 COSPE interviews, about employment services rather than the anagrafe, describe SPID as a hard step for first-generation migrants and for people who do not speak Italian well, and describe people paying intermediaries because digital access and waiting times get in the way. Whether a Milan resident who is changing address already has SPID is not measured here.

3. **Language help exists and still misses the reference user.** Milan publishes this service page in English and nine other languages. Turkish is absent. The 2023 Milan pilot for spoken help at the counter used Arabic, Spanish, and Russian, which were the languages most heard at that office. The vision currently promises English and Italian. That matches the English page. It does not match a Turkish speaker who cannot use either, and it does not match the languages the city itself staffed.

4. **The papers depend on facts the person may not have assembled.** A non-EU permit has to be attached. A tenant is told to have the lease details and the owner's identity card. In one 2023 r/Avvocati thread, a renter was told the Agenzia delle Entrate registration receipt has to be requested from the landlord. Milan's support article separates "enough to file" from "needed later so the family relationship is recorded." A person can file and still be missing the record that makes the household correct.

5. **The 20 days start at the move, and the case stays open after sending.** The duty is to declare within 20 days. Registration follows in two working days, then checks can run for 45 days. A negative check interrupts the procedure, and the effects that began on filing day can be unwound. Anecdotes on r/Avvocati describe a weekend visit when nobody was home, a rejection that the ANPR status still showed as accepted with reserve, and uncertainty about how to say "I am only home on the weekend." These are single reports. They show a failure mode: the person can believe the file is finished when the municipality does not.

6. **One move wakes up a second obligation.** Milan puts the TARI housing declaration on the same page as the address change. The masterplan already parks TARI outside the first release unless it is approved. Users still meet it at the moment they change address.

7. **A wrong declaration is costly.** Milan warns that a false statement is reported to the judicial authority, benefits can be lost, and criminal penalties apply. The product problem is that a helpful guess is the wrong kind of help.

## Assumptions

These are untested. An interview answer can retire any of them.

| # | Assumption | What would retire it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The painful case is an address change inside Milan by someone already registered, not a first registration or a permit renewal. | Interviewees mostly need a different procedure, or the address change is easy once the permit problem is solved. |
| 2 | The reference user can sign in with SPID or CIE, or can get one without PaperPilot. | They are blocked on digital identity before any form starts. |
| 3 | A registered lease and a cooperative landlord are available. | The usual blocker is a missing registration receipt or a landlord who will not provide it. |
| 4 | English or Italian is enough for the Turkish resident in the vision. | They need Turkish to understand the documents and the warnings. |
| 5 | People will upload a passport and a residence permit to a private assistant. | They will use the checklist and will not upload identity documents. |
| 6 | Official pages are confusing enough that an assistant is useful even though Milan already publishes an English service page. | People who can read English complete the ANPR form without help. |
| 7 | The anxiety is the filing. | The anxiety is the 45-day check, the home visit, and not knowing the file was refused. |

## Interview questions

Ask in the participant's language. Recruit people who moved home inside Milan in the last year, or who are about to, and who were not born in Italy. Ten conversations are enough to keep or drop the assumptions above.

1. What were you trying to change, and how did you learn the name of the procedure?
2. Which websites, offices, or people did you use, and where did they disagree?
3. Did you have SPID or a CIE before you started? What did it take to use it here?
4. Which documents did you have on day one, and which appeared only after you had started?
5. What did you need from the landlord or the owner, and did you get it?
6. Who else moved, and was anyone already registered at the new address?
7. After you sent the declaration, what happened during the checks? Did anyone call or visit?
8. Did the move force a TARI declaration, a Questura notice, or a permit renewal?
9. What would you have paid another person to do, and which documents would you refuse to upload?
10. Which language did you need, and was the city's English page enough?

## Open point for the vision

The English municipal page weakens the claim that official instructions are unavailable in English. The remaining language gap for the reference user is Turkish, plus the languages Milan actually staffed at the counter. Task 6 should decide whether English and Italian remain the first-release languages after these interviews, not before.
