# PaperPilot product vision

Task 1. This document specifies the problem, the primary audience, the value proposition, and the product objectives. It is the vision only. User research, competitor comparison, the supported-case list, scenario collection, and `PRODUCT.md` come in later tasks.

## Problem

Government procedures are scattered across offices, forms, and languages. A person has to find the right procedure, the official page, the eligibility rules, the deadlines, and the documents, then keep track of all of it until the application is submitted. Official websites publish general instructions. PaperPilot has to turn one person's situation into the applicable path, the missing facts, and a case that lasts until they submit.

This is hardest for people who do not already know the local administrative system or its language: international students, immigrants, and expatriates.

## Primary audience

The first release is for people in Milan who must change their registered residential address and do not already know that procedure.

The reference person is a foreign resident who has moved to a new apartment and must update that registered address. The brief's example is a Turkish citizen living in Milan. International students, immigrants, and expatriates in that situation are who this release is for.

## Value proposition

The person describes their situation in their own words. PaperPilot identifies the applicable procedure, cites verified official requirements, asks only for facts that are still missing, checks the documents they upload, and returns a checklist plus a submission package for them to review. They authenticate and submit through the official government channel.

## Product objectives

1. Select the correct supported procedure from the person's circumstances, location, and goal.
2. State every administrative requirement with a verified official source, and mark a requirement when it is uncertain, conflicting, or unsupported.
3. Produce a personal checklist of documents, steps, deadlines, and dependencies, and point out missing or inconsistent information.
4. Keep the case, the conversation, the documents, and the remaining work across sessions.
5. Assist in English and Italian, including official administrative terms.
6. Leave government authentication and final submission with the user, and direct them to the correct official channel.

## Boundary

The first procedure in view is a change of registered residence address in Milan, in English and Italian. Supported and excluded cases are listed in `MVP_SCOPE.md`. Success metrics are in `PRODUCT.md`. This document does not set screen designs or system architecture.
