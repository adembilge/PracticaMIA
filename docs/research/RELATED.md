# Related procedures

Task 13. Written on 2 November 2026. SRC-10 was opened that night. SRC-06 and SRC-11 were opened again the same night. Source ids are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). The habitual-residence clock is [DL-11](DEADLINES.md).

A row is another procedure an opened page connects to an address change. Directly dependent would mean the page says the address-change declaration itself does not finish without it. None of these pages says that. Awareness means the page tells the person about a separate act, or about something registration makes possible. Every row stays outside this release and does not open a case.

This file is not the dossier, and it is not a rule the agent may state. Task 14 is still the human review. The review status of every row is unreviewed.

## Not a follow-up of this change

SRC-06 lists four other services under "Servizi correlati": the form for a foreign national arriving from abroad, a transfer of residence abroad for an Italian citizen, ResidenzaMi for a person without a home, and a certificate of cancellation for untraceability. The page does not say that any of them follows an intra-Milan change. They are not rows. The from-abroad form is already CH-10.

SRC-11's registration of an EU citizen in order to obtain a stay certificate is DL-14. It is not a follow-up of an intra-Milan change.

SRC-06 cites citizenship statutes under a heading of its own. It does not say a citizenship request follows the address change. Those statutes were not opened.
