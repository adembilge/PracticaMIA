# Document requirements

Task 10. Written on 1 November 2026. SRC-01, SRC-02, SRC-06, SRC-07, SRC-09, and SRC-11 were opened again that night. Source ids are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). The sequence is in [PROCEDURE.md](PROCEDURE.md). The branches are in [ELIGIBILITY.md](ELIGIBILITY.md).

A row is a document or a form field one of those pages names. [MVP_SCOPE.md](../product/MVP_SCOPE.md) says whether the first release asks for it. It is not the official rule. Deadlines, sign-in, and TARI stay in Tasks 11–13. This file is not a rule the agent may state. Task 14 is still the human review. The review status of every row is unreviewed.

Where two pages do not use the same words, both are kept. `Example` means the page gives an instance, not a closed list. `Not stated` means the page names the item and does not say that it is mandatory, conditional, alternative, or optional.

## What the ANPR form asks for

SRC-01 says the form asks for the people who are moving, the new address, the title to occupy the home, and contact details, and then for attachments. It does not name the family or address fields below. Those names are in SRC-02. SRC-01's "Cosa serve" section says the person will be asked for some information, and its examples are the cadastral details when the home is theirs, or the lease details and the owner's identity card when it is not. Those examples are not a complete list.

## What the other two pages add

These rows are not folded into the ANPR rows above, because the pages are not describing the same list.

SRC-07 is the Milan note on documents for a residence request when the permit is valid. It does not say the list is the ANPR attachment step. Two of its sentences do not agree: it first says the civil-status papers are necessary, and then says they are not obligatory. Both sentences stay.

SRC-11 describes an anagrafic application presented in person or sent to the municipality. It says a copy of the applicant's identity document, and of the identity document of each person moving with them, must be attached, and that adults sign the form. That is a different channel from the ANPR attachment step. For a first registration from abroad it says the foreign national must show a valid passport or equivalent document, with an exception for asylum seekers and holders of international protection. For every other anagrafic declaration it says showing a recognition document is enough: a document with a photograph, on paper or on a magnetic or computer support, issued by an Italian public administration or by another state, that identifies the holder.

## Opened, and not a starting document

SRC-06 does not list the papers or the form fields for the declaration. Its sentence about memories or documents is the reply after a negative check. That reply is already in the procedure file. It is not a row here. The statutes and circulars SRC-06 cites were not opened. SPID and CIE are Task 12. TARI is Task 13.

SRC-07 links other Milan notes, including the guest-housing note, the note for a permit that is being renewed, and the note for a first permit that has not been issued. Those pages were not opened. The short text on the SRC-07 cards is not a row.

The guide names a later state, "Sospesa", in which the municipality can ask for more documents. It does not list those documents. There is no row for them.

## Product boundary

The first release checks an uploaded copy of the lease or of the ownership paper, and an identity or permit document, as stated in `MVP_SCOPE.md`. That check is the product boundary. It does not add a row. The ANPR pages call the permit attachment obligatory only for citizenship outside the EU, and they call the other attachments optional. The lease details and the owner's identity card on SRC-01 are examples.
