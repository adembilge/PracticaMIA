# Eligibility branches

Task 9. Written on 1 November 2026. SRC-01, SRC-06, and SRC-08 were opened again that night. SRC-02 is the citizen guide opened earlier the same day. Source ids are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). The sequence is in [PROCEDURE.md](PROCEDURE.md).

A row is a circumstance one of those pages states. [MVP_SCOPE.md](../product/MVP_SCOPE.md) says whether the first release opens a case. It is not the official rule. Document fields, deadlines, and sign-in methods are later tasks.

The first release opens a case only when the residence, household, and tenancy rows below all say it does. One matching row is not enough.

## Residence

SRC-01 addresses people already registered in the Anagrafe Nazionale. SRC-06 does not say that. Both name the first three rows. The disagreement stays open.

| Branch | What the opened page says | First release |
|---|---|---|
| Same comune | SRC-01: a change of home inside the same comune. SRC-06: a change of address inside Milan. | Opens a case only for a person already registered in Milan whose new address is also in Milan, and only when the household and tenancy rows also match. |
| Another Italian comune | SRC-01: a move to another comune. SRC-06: arrival from another Italian comune. | Does not open a case. The ANPR service still names this path. |
| AIRE return | SRC-01 and SRC-06: an Italian citizen registered with AIRE who has returned to Italy. | Does not open a case. The ANPR service still names this path. |
| Foreign national arriving from abroad | SRC-08: foreign people arriving from abroad. SRC-06 links to that page. SRC-02: this declaration goes to the municipal desk. | Does not open a case. The two doors are not reconciled here. |
| Convivenza | SRC-02: a move into a convivenza goes to the municipal desk. SRC-01 does not name it. | Does not open a case. |
| Guardian | SRC-02: a declaration by a guardian goes to the municipal desk. SRC-01 does not name it. | Does not open a case. |

SRC-02 was not linked from the live citizen page on this date. The convivenza and guardian rows rest on that guide.

## Nationality

| Branch | What the opened page says | First release |
|---|---|---|
| Italian | SRC-06 includes Italian people on the ANPR path. The AIRE row above is limited to Italian citizens. | Can fit the supported case when the other rows match. |
| Foreign | SRC-06 includes foreign people on the same ANPR path as Italian people. SRC-01 does not split the same-comune and other-comune paths by nationality. | Can fit the supported case when the other rows match. |
| Non-EU | SRC-01 says a person with citizenship outside the EU must attach the residence permit. It does not say the service refuses to start without one. | Does not open a case when there is no permit, or the permit cannot be attached. The papers themselves are Task 10. |
| EU-only | No opened page states a separate rule for an EU citizen. SRC-06 cites Directive 2004/38/CE and D.Lgs. 30/2007, and those were not opened. | No EU-only rule is stated. An EU citizen is in the foreign row above. |

## Household

| Branch | What the opened page says | First release |
|---|---|---|
| Nuova residenza | SRC-01: a home where nobody else lives, or where the other people are not relatives or partners. The page's example is students sharing an apartment. | Can be the supported case when the person files only for themselves and the tenancy row matches. The student example is not itself a case. |
| Residenza in famiglia esistente | SRC-01: a home where the other people are relatives or partners. The page's example is a couple. | Does not open a case. |
| Only the declarant, or the only adult, is moving | SRC-01: that person sends the declaration. SRC-02's summary section for this case matches. | Opens a case only when the person files for themselves alone. |
| Other adults are moving too | SRC-01: each of those adults confirms in their own ANPR area, and the declaration is sent when all of them have confirmed. | Does not open a case. |

An opening sentence of SRC-02 says every adult must confirm, without the split in the last two rows. The guide's later summary sections match SRC-01. This file does not choose which sentence governs.

SRC-01 lets the declarant include members of the current registered family. The first release still requires that the person file only for themselves, so a declaration that includes anyone else does not open a case. No opened page states an age minimum for the declarant. The product boundary of 18 or older is not an official branch.

## Tenancy

SRC-01 says the form asks for the title to occupy the home. Its examples are the owner, and a person for whom the home is not their own. It does not list the six titles below.

SRC-02 lists these titles. They were not on the live citizen page.

| Branch | What SRC-02 says | First release |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | The declarant is the owner of the home. | Opens a case when the residence and household rows also match. |
| Named holder of a registered lease | The declarant is the holder of a regularly registered lease. | Opens a case when the residence and household rows also match. |
| Public-housing lease | The declarant is the holder of a public-housing lease. | Does not open a case. |
| Registered comodato | The declarant is the holder of a regularly registered free-loan contract. | Does not open a case. |
| Usufruct | The declarant is the usufructuary. | Does not open a case. |
| Another title | The declarant describes some other legitimate title in the notes. | Does not open a case. The product also excludes occupying the home only as a guest. The guide does not use that word. |
