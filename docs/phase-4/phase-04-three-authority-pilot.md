# Phase 4 pilot scope and source review

**Prepared 20 November 2026.** These three candidate cases define the scope for [Task 40](phase-04-agent-and-conversation-engine.md#40-evaluate-the-agent-and-close-phase-4). The scope awaits the product owner's decision. The repository records zero participant sessions for this pilot. Milan's signed first route is the baseline. The [acquisition record](phase-04-pilot-source-acquisition-2026-11-20.md) pins passages for T1–T3 and Q1–Q3. Q3 covers only the appointment details supplied in the applicant's postal summons. Local office guidance and all human decisions on the new claims remain pending.

## Candidate cases

| Case | Context to establish | Competent body and next action to verify | Limits of the case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Milan residence change** | An adult Italian citizen already registered in Italy moves alone into Comune di Milano, is the named holder of a registered private residential lease, and will join no existing household. The owner confirms the saved intake. | Comune di Milano, Ufficio Anagrafe receives the declaration. The applicant can continue to ANPR through the existing signed handoff. | Use the [signed first-route scope](../../docs/MILAN_FIRST_ROUTE_OWNER_REVIEW_ACTIVATION_2026_11_08.md#decision-scope-and-limitations). Current attachment requirements and portal state require their own evidence. |
| **Torino marriage banns** | Both adults are registered residents of Torino and report never having married. They plan a civil ceremony in another named municipality. The resident is asking where to start the banns. | Città di Torino, Ufficio pubblicazioni di matrimonio handles the banns request through Torino's online service. The civil registrar sends the delegation to the ceremony municipality after publication. | Establish registered residence and ceremony municipality separately. The delegation is a later office action. The case ends at the banns service. |
| **Milano Questura appointment after postal renewal** | An adult who lives and is registered in the province of Milano reports lodging a code 16 employment-permit renewal via Sportello Amico. A fictional summons names the Milano Questura immigration office and provides appointment details. | Questura di Milano, Ufficio Immigrazione handles the post-filing appointment. The applicant follows the venue and time stated in the summons. The enabled post office handled the earlier intake. | Treat the stated filing and summons as case inputs. Refer to the summons for venue and time. The case ends at explaining the appointment step and leaves permit status unresolved. |

The coverage count uses the competent body for each case's target step. ANPR is the channel for Milan's declaration. Poste Italiane is the postal intake intermediary for the selected permit case. The ceremony municipality's delegation role is downstream of Torino's banns request.

3. Record whether the participant independently reached and understood the expected action, the authority and passage IDs shown, any misroute, clarification and resumption result.
